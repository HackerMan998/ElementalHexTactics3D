High Concept: Why Make This Game?
Inspirations & Core Idea
Final Fantasy Tactics + Into the Breach + MtG Land-Tapping
The problem with classic tactics: Battles often take 40+ minutes, and the terrain is just flat scenery with a generic +10% evasion stat.
  • Dynamic Grid: Spells physically change the ground. Fire turns grass into burning lava; water puts out fires and makes steam.
  • Satisfying Knockbacks: Shove enemies into walls for heavy impact damage, or push them straight into lava.
  • Land-Harvesting: Drain active lava or water into barren stone to power up devastating Titan spells.

🎯 Fast, Puzzle-Like Skirmishes

Instead of endless menus and slow math, each unit gets a clean 1 Move + 1 Action turn. Battles are tight 10-minute spatial puzzles that respect the player's time.

💡 Clear Visual Feedback (Ghost UI)

Hovering a spell shows exactly how the terrain will react before committing, so players can plan clever chain reactions without getting confused.

Speaker Note: "I love FFT and Tactics Ogre, but I always wished the terrain did more. In Elemental Hex Tactics, combat feels like a punchy puzzle: you light the ground on fire, shove enemies into hazards, and harvest that energy for big finishers."
Combat Loop: The 5-Step Turn Flow
Core Gameplay Loop

1. Infuse

Cast Fireball on grass to scorch it into glowing, hazardous lava.

2. Shove

Push an enemy into a stone cliff for slam damage, or knock them into lava.

3. Attune

Stand on matching tiles to get buffs (Lava gives fire units +2 ATK and burn immunity).

4. Siphon

Drain active lava into Barren Earth to harvest Elemental Cores.

5. Cataclysm

Spend cores on the Titan's volcanic AoE to blow up surrounding enemies!

Natural Synergy: Every action naturally feeds into the next. Setting tiles on fire gives you hazards to push enemies into, buffs your fire units, and provides the fuel you need for your ultimate abilities.
Speaker Note: "This loop creates a really satisfying rhythm: you create the fire, kick the enemy into it, stand in it to buff your dragon, harvest that fire for energy, and then wipe out the rest of the board with a screen-shaking cataclysm."
The Squads: 2v2 Tactical Roles
Playable Units

🧙 The Commander (Player)

Role: Tactician & Board Controller.
Squishy but agile. Uses ranged fire spells to alter terrain, water blasts to create defensive steam, and kinetic pushes to manipulate enemy positioning.

🐉 FlameFrost Dragon (Allied Titan)

Role: Frontline Anchor & Finisher.
Heavy melee bruiser with 14 HP. Naturally immune to lava, controls the front lines, and siphons ground energy to cast the massive Magma Cataclysm.

🔥 The Dracomancer (Enemy Boss)

Role: Ranged Rival.
Commands the enemy squad, casts long-range fire spells, and tries to knock player units off high ground with his own shoves.

👿 The Demon Slime (Enemy Minion)

Role: Aggressive Swarmer.
Fast flanking unit that tries to hunt down your Commander and push him into dangerous terrain.

Speaker Note: "In our demo, the Commander and Titan complement each other: your Commander controls space and sets up the board from afar, while the Titan holds the frontline and executes enemies."
Visuals & Feel: 2.5D Standees & 3D Depth
Style & Polish

🎨 Retro Look, Modern Engine

Classic 90s sprite standees on multi-level 3D hex pillars with realistic rock cliff faces and glowing emissive lava veins.

🎥 Full Camera Control

Players can smoothly rotate 360° (Q/E), pan (WASD), and zoom to inspect line-of-sight and height differences from any angle.

💥 Punchy Combat Juice

Trauma-based screen shake on wall slams, floating damage numbers, and procedural sound effects that make every hit feel impactful.

Speaker Note: "We chose a 2.5D diorama look to capture the charm of classic tactics games, but with the benefits of Unity 6: 3D lighting, smooth camera rotation, and punchy impact effects."
